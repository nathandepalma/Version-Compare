CardioVale™ (velorstat) tablets

Designed for dependable blood pressure control

For adults with primary hypertension, CardioVale offers proven blood pressure reduction in as early as 2 weeks when used with diet and exercise.

Clinical profile

In the pivotal PACE-HBP study, CardioVale demonstrated superior systolic blood pressure reduction versus placebo at Week 12.*

CardioVale is generally well tolerated. The most common adverse reactions were dizziness and fatigue.

* PACE-HBP was a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in 842 adults with primary hypertension. See accompanying Prescribing Information.

CardioVale™ (velorstat) tablets

Designed for dependable blood pressure control

For adults with primary hypertension, CardioVale offers a clinically meaningful reduction in systolic blood pressure at Week 2 when used with diet and exercise.

Clinical profile

In the pivotal PACE-HBP study, CardioVale reduced mean seated systolic blood pressure by 14.8 mmHg versus 6.1 mmHg with placebo at Week 12.*

CardioVale is generally well tolerated. The most common adverse reactions (≥2% and greater than placebo) were dizziness and fatigue.

* PACE-HBP was a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in 842 adults with primary hypertension. See accompanying Prescribing Information.
